LAZY LOOPIN - AUTOMATIC CONTENT DISCOVERY

Replace the following in your existing project:
  app/page.tsx
  components/Library.tsx
Add:
  lib/getLibraryVideos.ts

Keep your existing data/videos.ts, app/globals.css, and public folder.
The archive includes the full project for convenience. Do not overwrite your public/content folders!

ADD A NEW VIDEO:
  public/content/My New Video/My New Video.png
  public/content/My New Video/result.mp4

No code edits required. The new folder appears after refreshing the site.
Optional category.txt inside the new folder: Watchlists, Movie Breakdowns, or Original Stories.
New videos without category.txt default to Original Stories.
Existing 15 videos keep their categories and TikTok IDs from data/videos.ts.
New videos have no TikTok button until you add their TikTok ID in data/videos.ts.
Hero carousel selects up to five videos, prioritizing the existing featured video and recent folders.

NOTE: This scans files present on the Next.js server. On Vercel, adding a local
folder requires a new Git commit/push and deployment. Large MP4/MOV files
may exceed Git hosting or Vercel deployment limits. Consider video hosting
for a growing library. MP4 H.264/AAC is the safest playback format.
